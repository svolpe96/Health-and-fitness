# Goals

## Long-term
- Earn a place on Team USA in powerlifting.
- Maximize total while remaining aligned with a lean, athletic physique.
- Build a data-driven training process that improves decision-making over time.

## Return-to-strength checkpoints
- Squat: 500 lb
- Bench press: 315 lb
- Deadlift: 585 lb

These are checkpoints, not end goals.

## Longer-term strength reference
Historical gym bests:
- Squat: 585 lb
- Bench: 355 lb
- Deadlift: 610 lb

The long-term objective is to exceed these while improving competition performance.

## Physique
- Preferred body-fat range: approximately 10–12%
- Well-developed shoulders/delts
- Clearly defined quadriceps
- Avoid unnecessary bodyweight gain or a deliberately thick/heavy powerlifting physique
- Keep hypertrophy work supportive of both performance and appearance

## Weight-class strategy
- Current class: 75 kg / 165 lb
- Do not push bodyweight upward simply for strength unless the performance tradeoff clearly justifies it.
- Nutrition strategy is intentionally deferred for now.
