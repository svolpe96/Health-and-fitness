# Coaching Decision Log

## 2026-10-05 — Establish return-to-strength phase

### Observation
Athlete has high historical strength but is returning from setbacks, with the squat most affected by a nearly resolved groin injury. Bench strength appears relatively well retained. Current deadlift capacity has not yet been calibrated.

### Decision
Begin a return-to-strength / reaccumulation phase rather than programming from historical maxes.

### Rationale
Historical best lifts do not represent current tissue tolerance or current 1RM. Early training should rebuild capacity, retain useful frequency patterns from the athlete's previously successful programming, and collect enough data to individualize progression.

### Initial logging rules
- Competition squat, bench, deadlift: RPE every working set.
- Accessories and non-competition work: last-set RPE by default.
- Track groin symptoms around squat/deadlift exposure.
- Do not change programming based on one poor session; look for trends.

### Success criteria
- Groin symptoms remain stable or improve as squat/deadlift loading rises.
- Working loads increase without disproportionate RPE escalation.
- Bench performance remains stable or improves.
- Sleep and recovery data become increasingly complete.
