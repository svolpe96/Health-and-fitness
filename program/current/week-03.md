Weights below are **planned, not completed**. If the session occurs late or after a missed day, do not cram sessions together. Use working-set RPE and symptoms to adjust loads. Do not treat prior strength as a current tested maximum.

## Logging standards
- Readiness: bodyweight, sleep, training time, hydration, caffeine amount/timing if used, pre-training energy.
- Competition lifts and main variations: RPE for every working set; optional mean concentric velocity (m/s) separately if measured.
- Accessories: actual sets, reps, load, and final-set RPE. Do not confuse target RPE with actual RPE.
- Record groin symptoms during and after squat/deadlift. If first set exceeds RPE target by >1, reduce load 2.5–5% for remaining sets rather than grind.
- No training performance should be marked completed until reported.
