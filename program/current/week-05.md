## Week objective
Continue squat strength progress with controlled RPE, consolidate Monday bench at 275 after Week 4's 7/8/8, progress Wednesday bench cautiously, and maintain conservative symptom-guided sumo loading. Week 4 Saturday was squat only (290x5x3 @5/5/5, groin 0 during/after); bench and accessories skipped, so do not infer their performance or add makeup volume. Current phase remains strength accumulation; no evidence-based reason for an automatic deload solely because four weeks have passed.

## Coaching and logging notes
- All loads are *planned*, not actual. Log actual weight, reps, and RPE for **each main-lift working set**. Accessories: actual sets/reps/load and final-set RPE.
- If first main-lift set is >1 RPE above target ceiling, reduce subsequent sets ~2.5–5% rather than grind. Do not force prescribed weights.
- For squat and sumo, monitor groin symptoms during and after. If symptoms exceed 2/10, worsen, affect technique, or linger afterward, stop the provoking movement and modify/omit. Persistent/worsening symptoms warrant clinical assessment. No pain threshold guarantees safety.
- Skipped exercises are marked skipped, not completed. No makeup volume. If short on time, prioritize main lifts then highest-value accessories.
- Readiness each training day: morning bodyweight, sleep hours; training time, hydration 1–5, caffeine mg/timing if used, energy 1–5.
