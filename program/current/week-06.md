## Coaching and logging notes
- Main lift actual RPE is logged **for every set**; accessories use last-set RPE. Distinguish target vs actual.
- If first set exceeds target RPE ceiling by more than 1, consider reducing remaining sets ~2.5–5%. Adjust based on performance and technique; loads are targets, not obligations.
- Squat/sumo: record groin symptoms during and after, and next-day response. If symptoms worsen, change technique, or persist, modify/omit the provoking movement; seek professional evaluation for concerning or persistent symptoms. Do not increase deadlift merely because RPE is low.
- Missing data is not a successful set. No makeup volume for missed sessions. Saturday is optional; its omission is not automatically evidence of poor recovery.
